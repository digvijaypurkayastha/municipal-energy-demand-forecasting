# Policy Brief: Municipal Grid Peak Demand & Conservation

## Executive Summary
Municipal power grids face load imbalances during peak thermal hours. Applying multivariate gradient-boosted forecasting enables municipal authorities to anticipate ward-level consumption spikes and automate targeted demand-response strategies.

## Key Recommendations
1. **Automated Load Shifting:** Target commercial HVAC loads during 10:00–14:00 peak hours using 3-hour lag predictive triggers.
2. **Ward-Level Allocation:** Dynamically distribute substation reserves based on microclimate variances and local occupancy rates.
3. **Continuous Evaluation:** Maintain automated drift detection on regional forecasting models to adapt to seasonal temperature transitions.
